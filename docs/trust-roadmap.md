# Trust & Credibility Roadmap

Following B2C user feedback (2026-03-18): site felt unclear about legitimacy for individual investors looking at solar land opportunities.

## Phase 1 — Quick Wins (High trust, low effort)

- [ ] **Footer overhaul**: add company legal name, address, email, phone number
- [ ] **Terms of Service** page + footer link
- [ ] **Privacy Policy** page + footer link
- [ ] **Contact info**: show email and phone prominently on homepage (not just Calendly)

## Phase 2 — Company Identity

- [ ] **About Us page**: company story, founding date, mission, vision
- [ ] **Founder/team section**: names, photos, credentials, LinkedIn profiles
- [ ] **Company registration info**: VAT / business number in footer

## Phase 3 — Social Proof

- [ ] **Add 3-4 more testimonials** from different use cases (B2C individual investor, B2B developer, landowner)
- [ ] **Partner logos / trust badges** section on homepage
- [ ] **Case study**: at least one real project outcome with metrics
- [ ] **Customer count / statistics** (e.g., "500+ parcels analyzed")

## Phase 4 — Authority & Transparency

- [ ] **"How it works" section**: clear step-by-step process on homepage
- [ ] **Data methodology page**: explain SunnyScore calculation, data sources, update frequency
- [ ] **Blog / insights**: solar market articles to establish domain expertise
- [ ] **Security & data handling statement**

## Context

Current trust signals:
- 1 verified testimonial (Manuel Falciatori / Soltec)
- Data source references in FAQ (FEMA, NREL, PAD-US, Natura 2000)
- Sample report modal
- Transparent pricing

Key gap: no visible company identity, no legal pages, no contact info beyond Calendly — individual investors see this as a red flag.
